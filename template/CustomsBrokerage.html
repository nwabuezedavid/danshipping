Customs Brokerage

CUSTOMS BROCKERAGE

We have an in-house team of licensed customs and trade professionals. They are experienced & trained in local Customs procedures and can provide advice on all import or export laws and regulations.

We facilitate Customs and Quarantine (MAF / AQIS) clearances for Airfreight, Oceanfreight, Courier and postal shipments at all ports throughout the world. These services include facilitating clearances with all the appropriate Agriculture, Forestry, Health and other Community Protection agencies.

We assist importers with such matters as tariff options, duty concessions, and landed costing reports. We also assist exporters with duty drawback and origin rulings. Other value added services include Technical Valuations, Legislation advice and general trade advice and consultancy.
